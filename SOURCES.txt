RESEARCH & CREATIVE NOTES — AZEROTH MORNING RADIO
Prepared for the Bright Worlds collection, October 2026.

METHOD
The research used public show pages, episode transcripts and show notes, official Warcraft pages, and established streaming-background examples. Audio-analysis attempts did not produce a reliable result, so this pack is based on text research rather than a claim of having listened through the episodes. Public automatic transcripts can contain spelling errors.

The ten folders cover recurring and occasional show features; they are not a claim that every segment appears in every episode. The hosts' current character races, classes, transmog, and exact in-game appearances were not sufficiently verified to build personalized character portraits. The Alliance emphasis follows the user's clarified direction.

GOBLIN NEWS NETWORK
The show’s news format receives eight fully goblin-built environments: Undermine, Bilgewater Harbor, Booty Bay, Gadgetzan, Ratchet, a nostalgic Kezan race pit, a zeppelin control room, and a Lost Isles radio outpost. Riveted metal, copper pipes, machinery, coin counters and a small goblin reporter carry the theme throughout the scenes. The goblin newsroom layouts and props are creative additions, not claims of real broadcast facilities in the game.
