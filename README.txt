START HERE
Open index.html in a browser to browse the collection by section. It starts with Goblin News Network. Click a picture for a large preview, then use the arrow buttons or keyboard arrow keys to compare choices. Every picture links to its original PNG.

The gallery works offline. Keep index.html next to the section and sticker folders. Extract the ZIP before opening the gallery.

BACKGROUND FILES
All 80 backgrounds are 1672 × 941 PNGs, a near-16:9 widescreen ratio. They are not native 1920 × 1080 or 4K exports. Fit them to a 1920 × 1080 canvas in your recording or editing application; the tiny ratio difference is negligible.

Each section has its own folder and eight numbered options. Every background includes faction imagery, with Alliance emphasized across the wider collection and Horde flags throughout Goblin News Network. The artwork uses bright lighting, Warcraft-inspired locations, and a few segment-specific objects.

The titles and flags are part of the image. There are no live hosts baked into this edition.

TWO REMOTE HOSTS
All eight Goblin News Network images have two equal framed areas in a goblin setting. Each host can record separately and be composited into one area.

The GNN images are opaque backgrounds, not overlays with transparent holes. Place the background under the camera sources. Either crop each camera to sit inside its framed bay, or remove each camera's real background and place the host in the scene. Frame locations vary slightly by artwork, so position them visually.

For the other sections, use one background for each host's camera, then arrange the two camera feeds in your existing split-screen layout. You can also use a single shared background with both hosts composited above it.

ANIMATION
A six-second silent Shifty reaction was created as a HeyGen generation request and shown in the chat's self-updating player. Requested motion: eyebrow lift, coin glint, sly wink, a teasing hover over the red button, and a small chuckle. Requested format: WebM with background removal.

The last tool-reported state was processing. The final video and its transparency have not been inspected or included in this ZIP. The player in the chat tracks the render. The clean Shifty PNG is included here as the source.
