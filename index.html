Bring the animated text into your show

Download and unzip the pack. In OBS, add a Browser Source, enable Local file, and select one of the six numbered HTML files. Set the source to 1920 × 1080 and place it above your cameras.

Each cue enters, holds, and fades away after six seconds. Enable Refresh browser source when scene becomes active, or use Refresh cache of current page in its properties to replay it. These are silent HTML overlays with a transparent canvas; the preview scenery is not included.

About this collection

This portfolio includes 80 backgrounds, 72 additional artworks (including both host sticker collections), and two dance clips. Early concepts are included to show the range of ideas; some differ from the final direction.

Warcraft-inspired fan concepts. Dance 2 is an animated interpretation of the in-game male goblin dance, not an exported game animation. Background research · Dance reference.
